Optional tools appear only after enabling them in Settings → Optional tools.

Disabled Market, Seller and Gil Trader no longer open an enable screen. Their menu entries, minimized icons and tool-specific settings stay hidden. Commands explain where to enable the tool without opening a window or changing your settings.

Disabling Market closes Market and Seller, stops repricing, removes highlighting and restores other plugins' pause/lock state. Saved records and pricing rules are retained. The bell opens Seller only while Market & Seller and Open Seller at summoning bells are enabled; it does not start price changes. Disabling Gil Trader closes it and stops the current session.

Camera tools now have an availability checkbox under Optional tools. Existing camera setups are kept on update. Disabling hides Camera settings and its shortcut, blocks /cam, and restores normal game height when safe. Your saved height, shortcut and saved-height preference remain remembered. QuickLoot and Helper retain their opt-in tabs and stop when disabled.

Both Goddess Blue and classic Dalamud use the same availability rules. Core tracking, gardening, pairing and saved records are unchanged. No Journal, Worker or Android update is required.

Validation: Release build, transition/sync regression tests, native tests and market tests. In-game check: disable Market & Seller, visit a bell and try /eqseller (no window); enable it in Optional tools and visit the bell again; minimize then disable Seller and Gil Trader and check that their icons disappear. Repeat with either design.
