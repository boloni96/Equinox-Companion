Equinox Companion 0.5.1.269 — cached retainers outside the bell

Inventory browsing no longer requires the game's live retainer list. It merges
this character's known market/observed retainers with Allagan Tools' public
GetCharactersOwnedByActive and GetCharacterItems IPC. Owned FC/house inventories
are excluded by container type; only retainer bags and crystals are included.
The owning character is checked before Allagan data is read. Names use native or
Companion records; unknown names temporarily show a retainer ID. Transient IPC
failures fall back to saved observations without discarding other retainers.

Recorded inventory refreshes once on opening or character change, and on explicit
Refresh/Preview. No per-frame history scans. Without a live roster, completeness
is conservatively unknown: known counts show a '+' in the retrieval preview,
while missing amounts stay Unknown. Retrieval still requires the actual bell
and validates its live retainer list before any withdrawal.

All items search marks craftable, workshop, or no crafting recipe. Ingredient
previews explain when a selected item has no recipe and is left unchanged. In the
reported screenshot the chosen augmented shoes were shown by the game catalogue
as No recipe; the planner must not invent crafting ingredients or silently replace
them with a different equipment version. Use Crafting logs to test a craftable item.

Validation: zero-warning/error build, gate tests including retainer-container
isolation and noncraftable behavior in all modes. Live acceptance: reopen away
from the bell and check cached inventory, then compare a Crafting logs item in
all three modes. No Journal deployment required; native withdrawals unchanged.

Additional fixes validated against the later screenshots:
- Full Attire entries resolve through MirageStoreSetItem into their actual pieces
  before ingredient planning and Artisan export. Exact mode keeps the outfit item.
- Recipes with identical ordinary materials/yield but different crystals/jobs use
  a stable recipe automatically. Its real crystals remain in the plan. Explicit
  recipe choices win; genuinely different material recipes still require a choice.
  Subcraft rows offer Recipe to change the job, and obsolete prompts are cleared.
- Any quality / NQ only / HQ only filters preview bag/retainer quantities AND native
  stack selection and completion counts. Presets remember quality; Clear resets it.
  HQ-only never substitutes NQ, even for crystals/non-HQ items. Inventory browsing
  remains a combined catalogue; the quality filter controls retrieval.
- The subcraft option is labelled List contains outputs AND their subcrafts to
  distinguish it from ordinary requested materials. Leave it off for turn-in lists.
- Artisan missing-output preparation explicitly uses combined NQ+HQ; the HQ filter
  is for retrieval and does not promise that Artisan will craft HQ outputs.

Local gate tests include 13 new cases for cached-container scope, noncraftable
items, outfit mapping, job alternatives and quality filtering/persistence.
In-game tests still needed: cached retainer view away from bell, Attire expansion,
NQ-only and HQ-only requests against mixed-quality stacks, and recipe override.
