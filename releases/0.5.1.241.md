# Equinox Companion 0.5.1.241

The Goddess menu now has a collapse/expand button halfway down its divider. Choose icons only or icons with names; the local preference survives window resizing, restarts and updates. Settings remains at the bottom, and every menu icon has a hover label. The setting is also available under Appearance.

Adventure character cards keep the portrait on the left, character details in the middle and house shortcuts on the right. At narrow widths house icons stack vertically in their right column. Portraits adapt to the available space; step headings and expanded work use the full width below the portrait. Missing location details are available on hover instead of taking up a placeholder row. My Empire, Person, Gardening and Submarines use the same right-aligned house controls. Switch/login labels shorten when necessary, with full information on hover.

Character and garden searches now keep their hint inside the field so it fits the content panel. My Empire's entry legend and gardening plan buttons wrap when needed. Includes the Goddess submenu-tab styling from 0.5.1.240.

Validation: existing transition/regression and native garden text suites, menu-preference save/reload checks, and Release build must pass before publication. In-game resizing and visual confirmation are still required. Garden detection, bed mapping, sync, login safeguards and travel requests are unchanged. No Journal or scheduled Worker deployment is required.
