Equinox Companion 0.5.1.251

Paired Market, retainer profiles and trading fixes

- Market now receives paired retainers and uses Journal Person / Account /
  Character identities. Current selling lists, visits, gil, job and level are
  shared. The newest observed stock wins, including empty lists; level/order
  updates do not pretend to be a fresh selling-list observation.
- Sales, purchases and price checks use separate authenticated, paged history.
  Confirmed updates remain distinct from suggestions, and game history replaces
  matching chat receipts/offline estimates without counting the sale twice.
  History download/upload and file work run outside the game update thread.
  Gameplay observations retain upload priority. Automation controls stay local.
- Background Universalis checks cover paired listings on their own home worlds.
  Hover explains Equinox check time versus upstream upload age; stale data stays
  uncertain. Background data never authorizes an automatic price write.
- Fix Gil Trader Send control sharing its ImGui ID with its direction selector.
  Add Party, Nearby, Friends and Find by name. Selection requires an exact nearby
  name/home-world match; active sessions remain bound to one game entity. Other
  players can use ordinary game trade controls without Companion. Start failures
  and button presses appear in Details and diagnostic exports.
- Distinct Market, Seller and Gil Trader icons in the launcher and minimized view.
- Submarines keeps person tabs fixed, removes an extra classification accordion,
  shows concise character/fleet rows and puts observation/slot details on hover.
  Fleet membership, detection, supply thresholds and voyage rules are unchanged.
- Welcome restores the Goddess dedication. Blue/classic choices open Companion;
  Close/X keep Blue without opening Companion. Both designs share all features.
