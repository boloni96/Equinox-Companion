Equinox Companion 0.5.1.37

The /equinox Planting tab now contains separate person subtabs, using the Journal's names such as Equinox Empire and Sir Haven. Each person has expandable Main/Alt account sections, then the same Regulars, Floaters, Empty and Pending sync groups used by the existing Persons housing views. Characters, houses and batches remain expandable.

Account grouping uses the existing account IDs, labels, character order and hidden-character preferences. Planting account expansion is saved independently from housing. Searching temporarily expands matching sections without replacing saved expansion choices. Each person has a separate scrolling area; unassigned garden records remain accessible in Other gardens.

Character name bars now include batch tending and harvest countdowns. Where individual batches do not fit, the bar shows batch count and earliest timers with due/ready warnings; hover shows each house and batch, owner or FC, address and last tending gardener. Click the bar to expand houses, batches and bed details. Shared houses can also be expanded from a linked character, and their timers are included alongside that character's owned gardens. Long labels are clipped to keep names and timers separate.

The shared website Journal, garden timers, owner/FC labels, house links and confirmed local action projection are unchanged. Both paired people continue to see all their linked accounts. Includes the previous harvest-to-empty fix.

Validation: release build succeeds with zero warnings/errors; 570 automated checks pass, including mixed ready/growing batches, overdue-tending priority, empty-bed clocks and estimated harvest state. The existing tested account/grouping helpers are reused. Native Dalamud layout remains for in-game review.
