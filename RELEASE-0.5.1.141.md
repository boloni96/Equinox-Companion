# Companion 0.5.1.141

The /bid world picker groups data centres under North America, Europe, Japan and Oceania headings. Data centres and worlds remain alphabetized within their own region, with expandable data centres. Selecting a region hides the other groups. Includes the Paissa-style column filters and current-world defaults from 0.5.1.140.

Validated with a local Release build. No Journal update beyond V7.11.166 is needed.
