Equinox Companion 0.5.1.256

Optional HQ/NQ crossover for manual price clicks

Market & Seller settings > Manual selling now includes:
  Allow HQ/NQ crossover on manual clicks

Off by default, preserving existing settings. Turn it on to explicitly use
an NQ comparison price for an HQ item, or an HQ price for an NQ item. It applies
to copying the adjusted price and filling the open editor. Existing undercut,
rounding, item minimum and click-confirmation settings remain in effect.
The item's quality and quantity are never changed. Known own-retainer prices
are matched exactly rather than undercut. A blocked cross-quality click now
explains the setting to enable instead of silently doing nothing.

Automatic pricing retains its existing quality, materia, history and increase
rules. The manual switch cannot enable cross-quality automatic suggestions or
updates. Settings now separate Manual selling, shared Price calculation, and
Automatic pricing rules. Both Companion designs use the same settings/actions.

Validation: 21 new manual-pricing checks, all existing market and paired-history
checks, transition/sync and native suites, plus the Release build. Covers both
quality directions, opt-in persistence, strict defaults, own-retainer matching,
invalid rows/prices, mannequins, minimums, rounding and automatic-rule isolation.
No live prices were changed by tests; in-game verification is still needed.

Test: with Confirm price after clicking off, enable the new manual option,
open an HQ item's price editor and click an NQ comparison price. Verify the
adjusted value appears in the editor/clipboard and the item stays HQ. Switch
the option off and repeat: it should explain why the price was not selected.

Companion-only update through the normal installer. No Journal, Worker or APK
deployment is needed; existing 7.11.244 packages remain current.
