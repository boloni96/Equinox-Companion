session. No position stream or Journal save is added. Lifestream may approach it
in a straight line (60 yalms maximum, vertical difference at most 5); no vnavmesh.

wrong world/map/territory, expired signals and invalid positions prevent replay.

including already rolled but unawarded items, plus five seconds clear and native
CanLeaveCurrentContent. Uses non-forced native exit; native behavior needs testing.
